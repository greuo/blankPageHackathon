# Discharge Companion design
Mode: Operate. The brief pins the visual world: white, mint, sky-blue, readable dark text, rounded controls and short purposeful motion. Code-first execution follows this detailed implementation brief; no separate visual approval is inferred.

## Design DNA
System: DM Sans body, Manrope headings, 16px body baseline, 1.5 line height; dark forest #173e35, secondary #53665f, active green #25715d, mint #e9f4ee, sky #eaf3fa, white #ffffff, canvas #f6f9f7; 4/8/12/16/24/32/48 spacing, 12–16px surfaces, 10px controls. Sidebar plus task-oriented work area, adaptive single column on phones. Explicit labeled states and focus rings.
Style: reassuring, unhurried, precise; a patient's personal recovery notebook supported by a clear clinician workspace. Transfer Notion reference's document hierarchy and restrained separators, never branding/assets. No statistics dashboard or marketing claims.
Effects: scoped GSAP completion timeline, 180–240ms panel changes; no WebGL/canvas/particles except exactly three decorative completion strokes. No automatic message animation, no page content hidden on load, reduced motion makes state changes static.
Allowed patterns: the five requested care-space cards represent five contexts for one fictional patient; pill labels only identify statuses; modal protects plan publication, permission changes, handoff consent or space focus.
